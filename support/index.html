提交问题时When reporting an issue

请尽量提供以下信息，避免包含私人乐谱、账户资料或其他敏感内容：

Please include the following when possible, and avoid attaching private scores, account details, or other sensitive content:

  • 使用的平台和系统版本
  • WA Score 版本
  • 问题发生前的操作步骤
  • 能够安全公开的示例文件或截图
  • Your platform and operating system version
  • Your WA Score version
  • The steps performed before the issue occurred
  • A sample file or screenshot that is safe to share publicly

常见检查Quick checks

MIDI

确认设备已经连接，并在系统中可被识别。报告问题时请附设备型号和连接方式。

Confirm that the device is connected and visible to the system. Include the model and connection method in your report.

Apple Pencil

进入“编写”，启用手写，在谱表区域完成笔迹后再执行识别。

Open Write, enable handwriting, draw inside the staff area, then run recognition.

iCloud

确认系统设置中已启用 iCloud Drive。关闭同步时，WA Score 仍会使用本地存储。

Confirm that iCloud Drive is enabled in System Settings. WA Score continues to use local storage when sync is off.
