WA Score 隐私政策WA Score
Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 22 日Effective date: September 22, 2026

概述Overview

WA Score 是一款本地优先的乐谱创作与编辑应用。开发者不通过本应用收集或出售个人数据，也不将个人数据用于跟踪、广告、营销或分析。

WA Score is a local-first music-notation and score-editing app. The developer does not collect or sell personal data through the app or use personal data for tracking, advertising, marketing, or analytics.

设备内数据On-device data

用户创建或导入的乐谱、文档和设置默认保存在用户设备上。Apple Pencil 笔迹识别在应用内完成，笔迹不会上传到 WA Score 开发者服务器。

Scores, documents, and settings created or imported by the user are stored on the user’s device by default. Apple Pencil handwriting recognition runs in the app, and handwriting is not uploaded to a WA Score developer server.

可选的 iCloud 同步Optional iCloud sync

用户可以选择通过自己的 Apple iCloud 账户同步乐谱资料。此服务由 Apple 提供并受 Apple 隐私政策约束。WA Score 开发者不能通过此功能查看用户的 iCloud 内容。

Users may optionally sync score data through their own Apple iCloud account. This service is provided by Apple and governed by Apple’s privacy policy. The WA Score developer cannot use this feature to view a user’s iCloud content.

账户、广告与分析Accounts, advertising, and analytics

WA Score 不要求创建账户，不包含广告、第三方分析 SDK 或跨应用跟踪 SDK，也不访问广告标识符。

WA Score requires no account, contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and does not access advertising identifiers.

数据控制与删除Data controls and deletion

用户可以在应用中删除乐谱和资源。卸载应用会移除应用本地容器中的数据；同步到 iCloud 的文件可由用户通过 iCloud Drive 或 Apple 账户设置管理和删除。

Users can delete scores and resources in the app. Uninstalling the app removes data from its local container. Files synced to iCloud can be managed and deleted through iCloud Drive or Apple Account settings.

Apple 服务Apple services

Apple 可能根据其自身政策处理 App Store、设备诊断和 iCloud 服务数据，此类处理由 Apple 负责。

Apple may process App Store, device diagnostic, and iCloud service data under Apple’s own policies. Apple is responsible for that processing.

政策更新Changes to this policy

如果应用的数据处理方式发生实质变化，本政策和 App Store 隐私声明将同步更新。

If the app’s data practices change materially, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated.

联系我们Contact

隐私或支持问题请通过 WA Score 支持页面提供的公开 Issue 渠道联系我们。请勿在公开 Issue 中提交私人乐谱或其他敏感资料。

For privacy or support questions, contact us through the public issue channel on the WA Score support page. Do not submit private scores or other sensitive information in a public issue.
